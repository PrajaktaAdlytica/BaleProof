BaleProof — Bale Witness identity

SVG masters are real outlined vector paths, including the wordmark: no embedded bitmap and no font dependency.
- horizontal: primary website and document logo
- stacked: centered cover or social application
- mark: full heron, bound material bale and evidence tag
- wordmark: typographic signature
- micro: simplified heron for small interfaces and favicons

Colors: burgundy #612d3d, charcoal #252b28, ivory #f5f2eb.
Use ivory on dark backgrounds. Preserve aspect ratio and allow clear space around the mark.
Use the micro mark below 40 px; use the detailed mark at 48 px or larger.
Favicon SVG plus PNG sizes 16, 32, 48, 180, 192 and 512 are included.
The full mark and wordmark were vector-traced from the user-approved concept. The micro mark is a simplified vector companion.
